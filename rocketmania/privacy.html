Rocket Mania – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Rocket Mania ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Le Nhung Team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app and the third-party services it uses collect, and the choices you have. The app has no user accounts and we do not run our own servers.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, gems, scores, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device (UserDefaults). We do not receive this data.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), IP address, device and app information, and approximate location, to serve and measure ads. In regions that require it (for example the EEA and UK) Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time via Privacy options in the app's info screen (shown where required) or in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See how Google uses data and Google's Privacy Policy. Upgrading to the ad-free version removes banner and interstitial ads.

Analytics and configuration – Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example screens and game events, app instance ID, device model, OS version, crashes and diagnostics) and Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings such as ad frequency. This data is not linked to your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app submits your scores and achievements to Game Center, which shows your player alias on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases (ad-free upgrade and gem packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Rocket Mania is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us at the email below and we will help you have it removed.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
