Privacy Policy

Speak a Little Daily (formerly EnglishVocab)
Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what data the Speak a Little Daily app (“the app”, “we”), published by Le Nhung, collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It also covers the older EnglishVocab app (version 1.x).

The short version

  • You can learn without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
  • Your voice recordings never leave your device. They are deleted after each answer.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store your learning progress so it can sync between your devices.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We do not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • We do not use analytics tools. We use crash reports to fix bugs; they are not linked to your identity.
  • You can delete your account and its data inside the app at any time.

Data we collect

If you learn without signing in (Guest). Your lessons, progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. If you delete the app or lose your device, this data cannot be recovered. Your device’s own backup (iCloud or computer) may include it, under Apple’s terms.

If you sign in with Apple or Google.

Collected automatically (all users).

  • Ads (free version). Google AdMob and its consent tool may collect a device identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic data. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Pro users do not receive ads.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with device and app diagnostics. Reports are not linked to your account.
  • Service data. The Firebase services we use (sign-in, database, configuration, abuse protection) receive technical data such as app version, OS version, language, country and IP address, to operate and protect the service.

We do not collect your date of birth, age, school, photos, contacts, precise location or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Speaking practice and speech recognition

  • The app uses your microphone only while you are recording an answer. Recordings are stored temporarily on your device, protected by iOS, and deleted after the answer is checked or cancelled. They are never uploaded to us and never included in backups.
  • To give feedback, the app uses Apple’s speech recognition. On devices that support it, recognition happens entirely on your device. On other devices Apple may process the recording on its servers; see Apple’s privacy policy.
  • You can turn off the microphone or speech recognition for the app in iOS Settings → Speak a Little Daily. Speaking activities then need that permission turned back on to continue.
  • The text recognised from your speech stays on your device while an activity is in progress and is deleted when it ends.

Ads, consent and tracking

  • Ads appear only in the free version, only between activities, never while you record or answer a quiz.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices. Declining never stops you from learning.
  • The app does not request permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency).

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check, Crashlytics) — sign-in, storing and syncing progress, app configuration, abuse protection, crash reports;
  • Google AdMob and its consent tool — ads in the free version;
  • Google Sign-In — signing in with Google;
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, speech recognition, App Store purchases.

Data is processed on servers of these providers, which may be outside your country.

How long we keep data

  • Account learning data: while your account exists. After you delete your account it is removed from our live systems within 7 days, and recovery copies expire within a further 7 days.
  • Server logs: about 30 days. Crash reports: about 90 days, not linked to your account.
  • Voice recordings: not kept (deleted after each answer).
  • Ad data: under Google’s policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. You need an internet connection and must sign in again to confirm. Your App Store purchases are not affected; you can restore Pro later, but not deleted progress.
  • Delete data on this device (Guest): Settings → Privacy → Delete data on this device.
  • Reset your progress: Settings → Reset Learning Progress.
  • Access to or a copy of your data, or other requests: email us (see Contact). We reply within 30 days.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: iOS Settings → Speak a Little Daily.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have further rights, including to object or to complain to your local data protection authority.

The older EnglishVocab app (version 1.x)

  • EnglishVocab 1.x stored your progress, and the display name shown on its leaderboards, in Firebase under an anonymous account.
  • Speak a Little Daily may read this data once on your device to show a summary of your old progress. That summary stays only on your device and is never uploaded.
  • We keep EnglishVocab 1.x data while version 1.x is supported, then delete it within 90 days. You can delete it earlier in Settings → Privacy → Old EnglishVocab data, or by emailing us.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for adult beginners. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data on your device uses iOS data protection; sign-in credentials are kept in the iOS Keychain. Data in transit is encrypted. Server access is limited to your own account by security rules. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to Other Sites

The app may link to other sites (for example ad destinations or the App Store). We do not operate them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes. If a change affects how we use your data, we will publish it here before the app version that introduces it.
