Third-party SDKs

Version 2.0 includes two third-party SDKs, added with Swift Package Manager:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 13.13.0) — loads and shows rewarded video ads when you choose to watch one to unlock a painting. Provided by Google under the Google Mobile Ads SDK terms.
  • Google User Messaging Platform SDK (version 3.1.0) — shows the ad consent form where one is required and the “Ad privacy options” entry in Settings. Provided by Google under its applicable terms.

These SDKs are not open-source software. The information they handle is described in the Privacy Policy and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Apple frameworks

The app also uses Apple system frameworks and Swift libraries:

  • SwiftUI and UIKit — interface, image rendering and system sharing.
  • Foundation, Observation and Combine — app services and state.
  • SwiftData — local progress, postcards and personal photo records.
  • PhotosUI and ImageIO — user-selected photo import and image preparation.
  • StoreKit — the Pro purchase and its restoration.
  • AVFoundation — gameplay sound effects.
  • WidgetKit — the daily puzzle widget, with its local snapshot shared through an App Group.

Apple system frameworks remain subject to Apple’s applicable terms. StoreKitTest and XCTest are used by the test targets and are not app runtime dependencies.
