Atlas Tile Puzzle, published by Le Nhung, is a sliding tile puzzle game featuring artwork collections from Vietnam, 3×3 and 4×4 boards, relaxed and challenge modes, a daily puzzle and streak, a collection map, an optional daily widget, collectible postcards and puzzles made from photos you choose. 3. Free chapter, rewarded ads and Pro

  • Free chapter: the “Miền di sản” (Heritage) chapter can be played without ads or purchases, painting by painting in order.
  • Rewarded ads: each painting in the “Miền Bắc” (North) and “Miền Nam” (South) chapters can be unlocked when the advertising SDK confirms that you earned the reward for that painting and the app successfully saves the unlock. The unlock is stored on this device until the app’s data is removed. Closing an ad before a reward is granted does not unlock the painting. Ad availability depends on Google and your connection and is not guaranteed.
  • Pro: a one-time App Store purchase that unlocks every painting in every chapter. Pro also removes the need to watch rewarded ads. There are no separate chapter-pack purchases or subscriptions. The purchase screen shows the price before you confirm.
  • Restore: “Restore purchases” restores Pro through Apple. It does not restore paintings unlocked with ads, deleted progress, photos or notes.

The app has no banner or interstitial ads. App Store payments, eligible purchase restoration and refund requests are handled through Apple under its terms.

Ad unlocks cannot be restored through Apple. Deleting the app or moving to another device without restoring a suitable device backup may remove these unlocks and your local progress.
