Privacy Policy

Atlas Tile Puzzle
Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy describes how Atlas Tile Puzzle (the “app”), published by Le Nhung, handles information in version 2.0.

At a glance

  • No app account or sign-in is required. Your puzzle progress, settings, imported photos, postcard notes and unlocked paintings are stored on your device. The app does not send them to us.
  • The app shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose “Watch an ad to unlock this painting”. There are no banner or interstitial ads.
  • Ads are non-personalized. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so it does not obtain your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). Non-personalized ads still involve the advertising data processing described below.
  • Google collects some device and usage information to serve and measure ads (see “Advertising” below).
  • Pro is a one-time App Store purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information needed to resume games and keep your collection:

  • Saved boards, puzzle and chapter identifiers, game mode, moves, elapsed playing time, hints and completion results.
  • Daily puzzle selections, saved daily games, completion dates and consecutive-day streaks, calculated using the date on your device.
  • Collected postcards, completion dates and the optional private notes you write.
  • Copies of photos you import, the titles you give them and their import dates.
  • Preferences such as tile numbers, sound, haptics, default game mode and whether onboarding has been completed.
  • The list of paintings you unlocked by watching a rewarded ad.
  • Your Pro purchase status, which Apple’s StoreKit verifies on the device.

These records are kept in the app’s local storage. The app does not provide an account, its own server backup or cross-device progress sync. iOS or iPadOS backups may include app data depending on your device and backup settings; those backups are managed by Apple or by you.

Paintings unlocked with ads stay on this device only. If you delete the app or move to another device without restoring a device backup, they are lost. “Restore purchases” restores Pro only, not paintings unlocked with ads.

Daily puzzle, map and widget

The daily puzzle and streak are calculated on your device. The collection map uses the locations of the paintings; it does not use your device location.

If you add the daily widget, the app shares a small snapshot with its own widget extension through an on-device App Group: the day, painting identifier, title, depicted location, thumbnail, daily completion status and streak. This is local sharing between Atlas Tile Puzzle and its widget, not an upload or sharing with another company. The widget does not receive your imported photos, postcard notes or purchase transactions, and does not load ads. Removing the widget stops it being displayed; deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any backups you manage separately.

Photos you choose

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker to receive only the images you select. It does not browse your entire photo library or request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. If a selected photo is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may need to download it to your device before the app can import it.

After you confirm the crop, the app saves a square JPEG copy for your puzzle. The saved copy is rendered from the image pixels and does not copy the original photo’s EXIF metadata, such as GPS coordinates. Information visible in the image itself remains visible. The original in Photos is not changed or deleted. Your imported images and their titles are not uploaded by the app and are never shared with advertisers.

Sharing postcards

A postcard is generated on your device and includes the artwork, its title, chapter and postcard date. Private notes and challenge results are excluded by default; you can choose to include them in the preview before sharing. The system share sheet sends the exported image only to the app or destination you choose. That destination handles it under its own privacy practices.

The app removes its temporary export file when sharing finishes or the preview closes normally. Copies you save or send elsewhere are outside the app’s control.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Paintings in the “Miền Bắc” and “Miền Nam” chapters can be unlocked one at a time by watching a rewarded video ad. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load and show these ads.

  • When the SDK runs: if you do not have Pro, the app checks ad consent when it opens. When Google’s consent status permits ad requests, the app starts the SDK and loads an ad in advance. Advertising data can therefore be sent to Google before you choose to watch an ad. An ad is only shown on screen after you tap “Watch an ad to unlock this painting”.
  • What Google may collect: device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version), your IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), ad interactions (such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped), performance and diagnostic data, and device identifiers that are not the advertising identifier. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud and measure ad performance.
  • Non-personalized ads: the app instructs Google to serve non-personalized ads only. Such ads are chosen using contextual information (such as approximate location and the app) rather than your past behaviour.
  • No tracking permission: the app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google through the app.

Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your ad privacy choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to check whether a consent message is required where you are, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If it is, Google shows a consent form before ads are requested, and you can make or change your choices. Where privacy options are required, Settings shows Ad privacy options so you can review or change your choice at any time.

You can play the free chapter without watching ads. Pro unlocks every painting without rewarded ads. Playing only the free chapter does not itself prevent the ad consent check or ad preloading described above.

Purchases and Apple services

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase that unlocks every painting. There is no subscription. Apple processes payment under its own terms; we do not receive your card details. The app checks your Pro status with Apple’s StoreKit on the device and does not send transactions to a server operated by us. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Apple may process App Store, device diagnostic or backup information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. This is separate from the app’s local game data.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please avoid sending personal photos or payment details unless they are necessary for your request. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

  • Game progress, daily records, streaks, settings, notes and ad-unlocked paintings remain in local app storage until you change them or delete the app’s data.
  • You can edit or clear a postcard note in its detail screen.
  • Deleting an imported photo in Library removes the app’s copy and its associated saved games and results; the original in Photos remains.
  • To remove all local app data, use Delete App in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Backups and exported copies must be managed separately.
  • Information collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s policies.

We cannot retrieve, restore or remotely delete local game data. Losing the device or deleting the app without a usable device backup may permanently remove that data.

Children and privacy requests

The same practices apply to all players. There are no app accounts or public player profiles, and ads are non-personalized. If a child contacts support, please avoid including unnecessary personal information. A parent or guardian may contact us about information sent to support.

You can contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. Depending on your location, you may also have other rights under applicable privacy law. Local game data can be managed on your device as described above; requests about advertising data can also be made to Google.

Security and changes

The app relies on the iOS or iPadOS app sandbox and device security. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure. We will update the date above when this policy changes.
